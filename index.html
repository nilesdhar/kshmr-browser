Enableton

Browse, play, and organize your Ableton Live projects and bounces — right from your Dropbox.

What it does

Enableton is a desktop app for music producers who keep their Ableton Live projects in Dropbox. It scans your project folders, reads each .als file for details like BPM, key, and track count, surfaces your bounces (MP3/WAV) for instant playback, and gives you playlists, notes, favorites, and version history across your whole catalog.

  • Fast browsing of every Ableton project in your Dropbox
  • Built-in audio player and waveform view for your bounces
  • Notes, favorites, playlists, and collaborator tracking
  • Optional email notifications when new bounces are delivered
  • A companion iOS app that streams the same library on the go

How Dropbox is used

The app connects to your own Dropbox account to read your Ableton projects and bounce audio, and to sync a small set of files (notes, favorites) between the desktop and iOS apps. It only accesses your files at your direction. Your project library stays between your devices and your Dropbox; Enableton’s limited backend handles licensing, free trials, and pre-release tester feedback as described in the Privacy Policy.
