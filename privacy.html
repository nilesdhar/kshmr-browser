Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the KSHMR Project Browser desktop application (macOS) and its companion iOS application (together, the “App”) handle your information. Please read it carefully. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

The short version

  • We do not operate any server, and we do not collect, receive, store, or sell your personal data.
  • The App runs entirely on your own devices.
  • It connects only to services you authorize — your own Dropbox account and, optionally, your own email (SMTP) account.
  • There is no analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party telemetry.

1. Information the App accesses

Files stored on your devices

The App reads your Ableton Live project files (.als) and audio bounces (MP3/WAV) to display details such as project names, BPM, key, track counts, and to play your audio. It stores a local catalog of this information (and your notes, favorites, and playlists) in a database file on your own device. This information never leaves your devices except through the services described below, which you control.

Dropbox

When you choose to connect Dropbox, the App accesses your own Dropbox account to read your project files and bounce audio, and to read and write a small set of sync files (for example notes and favorites shared between the desktop and iOS apps). Authentication uses Dropbox’s standard OAuth flow; your Dropbox login credentials are never seen or stored by the App — only an access token issued by Dropbox is stored locally on your device (in the macOS Keychain on desktop, and in the system keychain on iOS).

The App requests the following Dropbox permissions, each used only for the stated purpose:

  • files.metadata.read — list and identify your project folders and files.
  • files.content.read — read your .als projects and stream/download your bounce audio.
  • files.content.write — save the small sync files (notes, favorites) back to your Dropbox.
  • sharing.read / sharing.write — look up and create Dropbox share links when you choose to share a bounce.

The App accesses your Dropbox files only to provide its features at your direction. It does not transmit your Dropbox files to us or to any third party. Your use of Dropbox is also governed by Dropbox’s Privacy Policy.

Email (optional)

If you enable email notifications, the App sends email using your own email account via the SMTP server and credentials you provide. Those credentials are stored locally on your device (in the macOS Keychain) and are used only to send the messages you configure. We do not receive copies of these emails or your credentials.

2. Information we collect

None. The App has no backend service. We do not collect analytics, usage statistics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or any other personal information. Because we never receive your data, we cannot access, share, or sell it.

3. How your information is stored

All app data — your catalog, notes, favorites, playlists, settings, and access tokens — is stored locally on your own devices. Access tokens and email credentials are stored in the operating system’s secure keychain. Sync files you choose to create are stored in your own Dropbox account.

4. Third-party services

The only third-party services the App interacts with are those you explicitly connect: Dropbox (for file access and sync) and your own email provider (for optional notifications). The App does not integrate any analytics or advertising SDKs.

5. Your choices & control

  • You can disconnect Dropbox at any time from within the App, which revokes the App’s stored access token.
  • You can revoke the App’s Dropbox access directly from your Dropbox connected apps page.
  • You can disable email notifications and remove stored email credentials in the App’s settings.
  • Uninstalling the App removes its local data from that device.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for music producers and is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated “Last updated” date on this page.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at kshmrbrowser@gmail.com.
